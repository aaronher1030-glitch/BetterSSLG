## Summary

School-level memorandum conveying the Nationwide Online Survey on Digital Device Use in Schools, issued by the Vice President under the authority of the SSLG President in line with OM-NFSSLG-2026-001 dated 25 August 2026.

## Key details

- Directed to the Secretary, Treasurer, Auditor, Public Information Officer, Protocol Officer and grade-level representatives.
- The text sets the survey deadline as 4 September 2026 unless the National Federation extends it.
- The parental-consent paragraph appears only in some of the 27 August copies, not in the annex-bearing primary.
- A 1-page PDF export of the 27 August text is present.
