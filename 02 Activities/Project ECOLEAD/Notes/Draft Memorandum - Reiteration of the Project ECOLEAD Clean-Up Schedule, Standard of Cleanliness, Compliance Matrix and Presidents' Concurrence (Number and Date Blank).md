## Summary

Undated and unnumbered draft memorandum reiterating the twice-monthly Project ECOLEAD clean-up schedule, stating a verifiable standard of cleanliness, tabulating lapses and consequences in a Compliance and Sanction Matrix, and requiring every recognized club and organization president to sign an acknowledgment sheet. Number and date are left as placeholders until the issuance is assigned.

## Key details

- Header carries `No. ____, s. 2026` and a blank date line; every annex is labelled `OM-SSLG-2026-____`, so nothing asserts a number that has not been given.
- Schedule: every Friday of the Second and Fourth weeks, beginning 13 November 2026 to the end of term; dates move only by prior agreement with the assigned SSLG Monitoring Officer confirmed in writing to the Secretariat.
- Body clauses are set with literal house numbering (1 to 14), so the enforcement paragraphs read as numbered directives rather than a wall of prose.
- Annex A: area rotation for November 2026 – March 2027 as one compact table (6 pt, condensed so the whole rotation fits on a single page) carrying all 23 clubs, the five months and each month's assigned area on one grid.
- Annex B: SSLG Monitoring Officer Assignment Matrix, set on one page with all 23 assignments.
- The clean-up calendar is stated only by the clause 2 rule (Second and Fourth Fridays, from 13 November 2026); on the issuer's instruction the separate dates annex was dropped, so no monthly date table is printed and annexes run A–E.
- Annex C: five-item Cleanliness Verification Sheet, accomplished by the assigned SSLG Monitoring Officer in the presence of the organization president; the comfort-room item and the collection-point item were dropped because no segregation collection point exists. Attendance without a verified area is recorded as non-compliance.
- Annex D: acknowledgment and concurrence sheet laid out as a two-column printed signatory list — one signature line per organization with the club name printed below it, filled down the left column and then the right. The president signs at the SSLG Office; no sheet is returned by a deadline.
- Annex E: Compliance and Sanction Matrix, the enforcement detail moved out of the prose into a five-stage table — first unexcused lapse, second, third and subsequent, excused lapse, and false certification — each row showing what counts, who is notified, and the effect on recognition.
- The sign-off is not a separate page. Prepared by, Reviewed and Evaluated by and Concurred follow clause 14 on the same page, so the body closes with the signatures and the annexes follow.
- Superseding clause covers the start-date and scheduling passages of Memorandum No. 002, s. 2026 and Memorandum No. 003, s. 2026 once a number is assigned.
- Print copy rendered through Microsoft Word at nine pages with no blank pages; every table spans the full 512.7 pt text width with left-aligned cells, repeating header rows and unsplit rows. Annex A is condensed to 6 pt and Annex D's printed club names to 10.5 pt; the prose stays at 12 pt.
