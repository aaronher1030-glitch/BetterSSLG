## Summary

Memorandum directing the implementation of Project ECOLEAD, the LGCC school clean-up and environmental stewardship programme, to all club and organization presidents, advisers and SSLG officers.

## Key details

- Original 28 July issuance sets implementation from 31 July 2026 with twice-monthly clean-ups.
- Assessment uses a prescribed rubric on attendance and participation, with monthly recognition for the highest-performing organization.
- September copies restate the schedule and rotation with new start dates.
- The number OM SSLG No. 002, s. 2026 is also used by the 27 and 28 August 2026 digital-device survey memoranda, so the number alone identifies neither issuance.
