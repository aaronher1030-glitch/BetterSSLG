# School Club Classification Framework (Proposal)

A four-cluster grouping for the recognized clubs and organizations of Benigno S.
Aquino National High School, modelled on the four-way split used by Philippine
Science High School (Pisay) Main Campus. The cluster is an **operational** grouping
for planning and reporting; the official DepEd accreditation `Category` on each
club's form is unchanged.

> [!warning] Proposal, not policy
> Nothing in this note has been issued. It becomes binding only through a signed
> SSLG memorandum. Items marked *To confirm* are unresolved in the sources in this
> vault.

## 1. What the national issuances actually say

There is **no national four-category rule** for school clubs. The national layer
sets who may recognize a club, not how clubs are grouped:

| Issuance | What it fixes |
| --- | --- |
| DepEd Order No. 47, s. 2014 | SPG/SSG constitution. Art. XII creates a Coordinating Council of the presidents of all recognized co-curricular organizations — the body through which the learner government coordinates clubs. The Order does not itself say the student government supervises them. |
| DepEd Order No. 49, s. 2011 | Mandated programs, projects and activities of the student government. |
| DepEd Memorandum No. 55, s. 2021 | Recommended PPAs of the student government. |
| OUOPS No. 2023-03 (5 May 2023) | Interim Guidelines of the Learner Government Program — SPG/SSG become SELG/SSLG; election code and federation rules. |
| DepEd Order No. 9, s. 2026 | Three-term school calendar. Co-curricular activities may not run inside the instructional block. |
| DepEd Order No. 12, s. 2025 | Multi-year school calendar and activities. |
| DepEd Order No. 22, s. 2025 | Institutionalization of the School Sports Club in public schools (separate registration: Forms SSC.1 and SSC.2). |
| RA No. 11369 | National Students' Day. |

### What "institutionalized" means here

Only one school organization is called *institutionalized* in the national
issuances: the **School Sports Club**, under DepEd Order No. 22, s. 2025 (7 August
2025). The Order itself says it "guides school sports clubs' establishment,
operations, management, merits and rewards, fund sources, and monitoring," that
schools with an existing SSC "shall adopt the guidelines provided herein," and that
schools without one "shall establish their school sports clubs accordingly." Each
school has one SSC, Arnis is mandatory, and it took effect immediately upon issuance
and publication.

The other terms are not interchangeable:

- **Institutionalized** — the School Sports Club only.
- **Mandated** — YES-O, Barkada Kontra Droga, BSP, GSP and similar bodies, each
  created by its own DepEd issuance.
- **Recognized / accredited** — the general status of every club under DO 47,
  s. 2014 and the division accreditation process.
- **Established and operationalized** — the language of the still-draft club policy,
  which covers all clubs, not one.

**Consequence for Cluster C:** the school cannot run a second sports club beside the
SSC. Cluster C should hold the SSC itself, and the Sports, Health and Wellness Club
and the MAPEH Club must be checked against it — either one of them is the registered
SSC, or the SSC is separate and Cluster C has three members.

The only category field found in current **division practice** is on the club
accreditation form issued by the Schools Division Office of Valenzuela City for
SY 2026-2027 (DM No. 047, s. 2026, 5 January 2026, Enclosure 1, and the same list
repeated in its Constitution and By-Laws template). It offers five options:

1. Academic or Subject-Related
2. Advocacy and Awareness
3. Leadership & Governance
4. Faith and Belief
5. Interest and Talent

This is a **division-localized template**, adopted pursuant to DO 47, s. 2014. No
national issuance printing these five options was located, so treat the set as
current division practice, not as a confirmed national standard.

DepEd has also been drafting a national policy on the establishment and
operationalization of school clubs and organizations: first validation October 2024,
second validation May 2025, pilot implementation from January 2026, and feedback and
assessment October 2026. The October 2026 memorandum still speaks of "refining the
draft guidelines" and "further refinement and finalization", which is the basis for
saying no final Order exists — an inference from the documentary trail, not a
positive confirmation that none has been issued.

**Consequence:** a four-cluster scheme is a school-level policy. It is lawful under
DO 47, s. 2014, Art. XII, which creates the Coordinating Council of club presidents
through which the learner government coordinates them, but it must not overwrite the
five-option Category on the accreditation form. Keep both: the form records the
declared category, the cluster runs the school year.

## 2. The Pisay model

Pisay Main Campus publishes its clubs under exactly four headings:

| Pisay cluster | Sample clubs |
| --- | --- |
| STEM Clubs | ATOM, Computron, Math Club, Pisay.XYZ, Physics Youth Honor Society, SARS+, Astrosoc |
| Arts Clubs | Art Club, Bravura, Exposure, Himig Agham, Kamalayan, Likha, SaGala, SAMAPI, Sightlines |
| Sports Clubs | Alianti, Alyabo, Buhat, Flux, Kislap, Laab, Labuyo, Pallastro, Outdoor Club, Silakbo, Volant |
| Socio-Spiritual Clubs | ACTS, Aksis, Bake Club, Balamesa, CARE, Habitat for Humanity, Invictus, Orient, SCA, SERT |

What makes it work is not the labels but the rule behind them: **one club, one
home**, assigned by primary purpose, and used everywhere the school needs an
ordered list.

## 3. Proposed four clusters for BSANHS

| Cluster | Name | Covers | Pisay counterpart |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A | **AGHAM** — Academic & STEM | Subject, track and competition clubs; quiz, research, entrepreneurship, TVE | STEM |
| B | **SINING** — Arts, Culture & Media | Performance, visual arts, music, dance, publication and media | Arts |
| C | **LAKAS** — Sports, Health & Wellness | Sports, physical education, health and wellness | Sports |
| D | **BAYANIHAN** — Socio-Civic, Service & Spiritual | Environment, anti-vice, disaster resilience, red cross, scouting, faith and service | Socio-Spiritual |

### Rules

1. **One club, one home.** A club is assigned by primary purpose, not by adviser
   department.
2. **Cross-cluster work is a joint project**, not a second home. A club keeps one
   cluster all year.
3. **Assignment is made at accreditation** by the LGCC, on the recommendation of the
   club adviser, and recorded on the club's registry entry.
4. **Tie-break:** the adviser's department, then the cluster with fewer clubs.
5. **Not clustered:** the SSLG (it chairs the LGCC and sits above the clusters), the
   school paper if separately constituted, and the SPTA.

The form keeps the five-option answer. The cluster is written on the registry line,
not in the Category box.

## 6. Where the cluster is used

| Process | How the cluster applies |
| --- | --- |
| GPOA / CAPA | PPAs filed and consolidated by cluster; one calendar per cluster before the LGCC merge |
| Club Fair | Four booth zones, one per cluster, with a cluster marker on the map |
| Election of club officers | Election schedule released by cluster to avoid same-day clashes |
| Induction ceremony | Program order and seating by cluster |
| Project EcoLead | Area rotation and the monthly Outstanding EcoLead Organization award computed per cluster |
| Project TINIG | Public directory grouped by cluster |
| Budget and tokens | One line per cluster for shared items |
| Monitoring | One summary page per cluster in each ACR |

## 7. Draft policy language

> **Subject: Classification of Recognized School Clubs and Organizations into Four
> Clusters for S.Y. 2026-2027**
>
> 1. Pursuant to DepEd Order No. 47, s. 2014, Art. XII, which creates the Learner
> Government Coordinating Council of the presidents of all recognized co-curricular
> organizations, the channel through which the learner government coordinates them,
> and to SSLG Memorandum No. 001, s. 2026, which established the standing committees
> of the Learner Government Coordinating Council, all recognized clubs and organizations
> are hereby grouped into four clusters: AGHAM (Academic and STEM), SINING (Arts,
> Culture and Media), LAKAS (Sports, Health and Wellness), and BAYANIHAN (Socio-Civic,
> Service and Spiritual).
>
> 2. Each club shall belong to one cluster only, assigned upon accreditation by the
> LGCC on the recommendation of its adviser. The cluster shall govern the sequencing
> of the General Plan of Action, the Club Fair, the election schedule, the EcoLead
> area rotation, the annual awards, and the public directory.
>
> 3. This classification is operational and does not replace the category declared
> in the club's accreditation form, which shall continue to follow the DepEd
> category options.
