   [106] OM-SSLG-2026-001 - GUIDELINES FOR THE LEARNER GOVERNMENT COORDINATING COUNCIL ESTABLISHMENT OF STANDING COMMITTEES AND THEIR COMPOSITION FOR S.Y. 2026-2027
   [107] GENERAL PROVISIONS
   [108] All Officers of the Supreme Secondary Learner Government (SSLG) are hereby directed to establish functional Standing Committees within the school to ensure efficient, transparent, and responsive student governance.
   [109] The establishment and composition of these committees shall be aligned with the provisions of the SSLG Constitution and By-Laws, and anchored on the principles of participatory leadership, accountability, and service-oriented gove
   [110] The SSLG shall establish the following school-based standing committees: a. Executive Committee; b. Internal Affairs Committee; c. Secretariat Committee; d. Finance and Logistics Committee; e. Publicity and Technical Committee; an
   [111] The SSLG President shall issue a local school memorandum or resolution to guide the student body, class officers, and SSLG advisers.
   [112] The SSLG Vice President shall oversee the organization of these school-based committees, while ensuring an equitable distribution of responsibilities among all elected SSLG officers and grade-level representatives.
   [113] COMPOSITION OF COMMITTEES
   [114] Each Standing Committee shall have the following minimum composition:
   [115] The Executive Committee shall compose of the President as the Chairperson, Vice President as the Co-Chairperson, Secretary as the Secretary General, and the rest of the officers are members.
   [116] The Internal Affairs Committee shall compose of the Vice President as the Chairperson, Secretary as the Secretary General, and the rest of the officers are Members.
   [117] The Secretariat Committee shall compose of the Secretary as Chairperson and 1-2 Members (from the Representatives).
   [118] The Finance and Logistics Committee shall consist of the Treasurer as the Chairperson, Auditor as the Co-Chair, and 1-2 Members (from the Representatives).
   [119] The Publicity and Technical Committee shall consist of the Public Information Officer as the Chairperson, one (1) Co-Chair, and 1-3 Members (from the Representatives).
   [120] The Learner Welfare and Policy Management Committee shall consist of the Protocol Officer as Chairperson and 1-2 Members (from the Representatives).
   [121] The Chairperson of each committee shall designate or appoint the members from among the Representatives of the SSLG, based on competence, availability, and relevance of function.
   [122] As an alternative, the Chairperson may open committee positions to interested Representatives, subject to a screening process to ensure suitability and equitable participation.
   [123] No Officer shall serve as Chairperson, Co-Chairperson, Secretariat, or Member in more than one (1) committee, except when deemed necessary by the body to ensure functionality and continuity of operations.
   [124] The final composition of each committee shall be presented to and approved by the body through a formal motion or resolution during a duly convened meeting of the SSLG.
   [125] SPECIFIC STANDING COMMITTEES AND THEIR FUNCTIONS
   [126] EXECUTIVE COMMITTEE (EXECOM)
   [127] Make final decisions on proposed programs, projects, and activities.
   [128] Review and approve plans submitted by committees.
   [129] Set priorities and overall direction of the SSLG.
   [130] Resolve issues elevated by committees.
   [131] Ensure alignment of all committees with SSLG goals.
   [132] INTERNAL AFFAIRS COMMITTEE
   [133] Monitor progress of committee tasks and deliverables.
   [134] Follow up pending outputs and deadlines.
   [135] Track participation and involvement of officers.
   [136] Provide performance updates to leadership.
   [137] Address internal coordination concerns.
   [138] SECRETARIAT COMMITTEE
   [139] Prepare meeting agendas in coordination with leadership.
   [140] Record minutes and document discussions.
   [141] Compile and organize reports and official documents.
   [142] Handle official communications (letters, notices, endorsements).
   [143] Maintain organized records and filing systems.
   [144] FINANCE AND LOGISTICS COMMITTEE
   [145] Prepare and manage budget proposals.
   [146] Monitor expenses and ensure proper fund utilization.
   [147] Handle financial documentation and reporting.
   [148] Procure materials and resources needed for activities.
   [149] Ensure readiness of logistical requirements (venue, equipment, supplies).
   [150] Assist in physical setup and arrangement of materials during activities.
   [151] PUBLICITY AND TECHNICAL COMMITTEE
   [152] Design and release publication materials (posters, announcements, captions).
   [153] Manage official social media and information dissemination.
   [154] Document activities through photos, videos, and write-ups.
   [155] Handle technical setup (sound system, presentations, ICT needs).
   [156] Conceptualize and assist in venue design, decoration, and overall visual setup of activities.
   [157] Ensure that event setups are visually organized, presentable, and aligned with the theme.
   [158] Coordinate with the Finance and Logistics Committee for materials and execution.
   [159] F. LEARNER WELFARE AND POLICY MANAGEMENT COMMITTEE
   [160] Receive and document student concerns, complaints, and grievances.
   [161] Evaluate concerns and determine appropriate actions based on existing policies.
   [162] Provide policy and legal basis for decisions, actions, and recommendations, ensuring alignment with school rules, DepEd policies, and the SSLG Constitution and By-Laws.
   [163] Coordinate with school authorities regarding student-related issues.
   [164] Recommend appropriate interventions and resolutions.
   [165] Assist in implementing and disseminating learner-related policies.
   [166] Promote student welfare, discipline, inclusivity, and protection.
   [167] OPERATIONAL GUIDELINES
   [168] Committees shall conduct regular meetings and submit reports.
   [169] All actions and recommendations of committees shall be subject to deliberation and approval by the body.
   [170] Transparency, collaboration, and accountability shall be observed in all committee undertakings.
   [171] EFFECTIVITY
   [172] This Memorandum shall take effect immediately upon issuance and shall remain in force for S.Y. 2026–2027 unless otherwise amended or revoked.

   [114] The establishment and composition of these committees shall be aligned with the provisions of the SSLG Constitution and By-Laws, and anchored on the principles of participatory leadership, accountability, and service-oriented gove
   [115] The SSLG shall establish the following school-based standing committees: a. Executive Committee; b. Internal Affairs Committee; c. Secretariat Committee; d. Finance and Logistics Committee; e. Publicity and Technical Committee; an
   [116] The SSLG President shall issue a local school memorandum or resolution to guide the student body, class officers, and SSLG advisers.
   [117] The SSLG Vice President shall oversee the organization of these school-based committees, while ensuring an equitable distribution of responsibilities among all elected SSLG officers and grade-level representatives.
   [118] COMPOSITION OF COMMITTEES
   [119] Each Standing Committee shall have the following minimum composition:
   [120] The Executive Committee shall compose of the President as the Chairperson, Vice President as the Co-Chairperson, Secretary as the Secretary General, and the rest of the officers are members.
   [121] The Internal Affairs Committee shall compose of the Vice President as the Chairperson, Secretary as the Secretary General, and the rest of the officers are Members.
   [122] The Secretariat Committee shall compose of the Secretary as Chairperson and 1-2 Members (from the Representatives).
   [123] The Finance and Logistics Committee shall consist of the Treasurer as the Chairperson, Auditor as the Co-Chair, and 1-2 Members (from the Representatives).
   [124] The Publicity and Technical Committee shall consist of the Public Information Officer as the Chairperson, one (1) Co-Chair, and 1-3 Members (from the Representatives).
   [125] The Learner Welfare and Policy Management Committee shall consist of the Protocol Officer as Chairperson and 1-2 Members (from the Representatives).
   [126] The Chairperson of each committee shall designate or appoint the members from among the Representatives of the SSLG, based on competence, availability, and relevance of function.
   [127] As an alternative, the Chairperson may open committee positions to interested Representatives, subject to a screening process to ensure suitability and equitable participation.
   [128] No Officer shall serve as Chairperson, Co-Chairperson, Secretariat, or Member in more than one (1) committee, except when deemed necessary by the body to ensure functionality and continuity of operations.
   [129] The final composition of each committee shall be presented to and approved by the body through a formal motion or resolution during a duly convened meeting of the SSLG.
   [130] SPECIFIC STANDING COMMITTEES AND THEIR FUNCTIONS
   [131] EXECUTIVE COMMITTEE (EXECOM)
   [132] Make final decisions on proposed programs, projects, and activities.
   [133] Review and approve plans submitted by committees.
   [134] Set priorities and overall direction of the SSLG.
   [135] Resolve issues elevated by committees.
   [136] Ensure alignment of all committees with SSLG goals.
   [137] INTERNAL AFFAIRS COMMITTEE
   [138] Monitor progress of committee tasks and deliverables.
   [139] Follow up pending outputs and deadlines.
   [140] Track participation and involvement of officers.
   [141] Provide performance updates to leadership.
   [142] Address internal coordination concerns.
   [143] SECRETARIAT COMMITTEE
   [144] Prepare meeting agendas in coordination with leadership.
   [145] Record minutes and document discussions.
   [146] Compile and organize reports and official documents.
   [147] Handle official communications (letters, notices, endorsements).
   [148] Maintain organized records and filing systems.
   [149] FINANCE AND LOGISTICS COMMITTEE
   [150] Prepare and manage budget proposals.
   [151] Monitor expenses and ensure proper fund utilization.
   [152] Handle financial documentation and reporting.
   [153] Procure materials and resources needed for activities.
   [154] Ensure readiness of logistical requirements (venue, equipment, supplies).
   [155] Assist in physical setup and arrangement of materials during activities.
   [156] PUBLICITY AND TECHNICAL COMMITTEE
   [157] Design and release publication materials (posters, announcements, captions).
   [158] Manage official social media and information dissemination.
   [159] Document activities through photos, videos, and write-ups.
   [160] Handle technical setup (sound system, presentations, ICT needs).
   [161] Conceptualize and assist in venue design, decoration, and overall visual setup of activities.
   [162] Ensure that event setups are visually organized, presentable, and aligned with the theme.
   [163] Coordinate with the Finance and Logistics Committee for materials and execution.
   [164] F. LEARNER WELFARE AND POLICY MANAGEMENT COMMITTEE
   [165] Receive and document student concerns, complaints, and grievances.
   [166] Evaluate concerns and determine appropriate actions based on existing policies.
   [167] Provide policy and legal basis for decisions, actions, and recommendations, ensuring alignment with school rules, DepEd policies, and the SSLG Constitution and By-Laws.
   [168] Coordinate with school authorities regarding student-related issues.
   [169] Recommend appropriate interventions and resolutions.
   [170] Assist in implementing and disseminating learner-related policies.
   [171] Promote student welfare, discipline, inclusivity, and protection.
   [172] OPERATIONAL GUIDELINES
   [173] Committees shall conduct regular meetings and submit reports.
   [174] All actions and recommendations of committees shall be subject to deliberation and approval by the body.
   [175] Transparency, collaboration, and accountability shall be observed in all committee undertakings.
   [176] EFFECTIVITY
   [177] This Memorandum shall take effect immediately upon issuance and shall remain in force for S.Y. 2026–2027 unless otherwise amended or revoked.
