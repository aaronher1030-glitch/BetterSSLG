### Important

**I1 — `om-sslg-2026-003-ecolead` note claims a retained rubric its own primary does not contain (and the vault elsewhere says the opposite).**
Note line `02 Activities/Project ECOLEAD/Notes/Memorandum No. 003, s. 2026 - Revised Implementation of Project ECOLEAD.md:47` — "Retains the rotation schedule and rubric-based assessment."
Measurement (case-insensitive counts over `word/document.xml`, tag-stripped): primary `02 Activities/Project ECOLEAD/Sources/OM_SSLG_2026_002 (7).docx` → rubric 0, assessment 0, criteria 0, recognition 0, certificate 0, rotation 3. `02 Activities/Project ECOLEAD/Sources/OM_SSLG_2026_002.docx` (the No. 002 issuance) → rubric 2, assessment 2, recognition 1, certificate 1, rotation 3.
So rotation is retained but the rubric is not present in the No. 003 text. This directly contradicts the vault's own `review_reasons` for `om-sslg-2026-002-ecolead`, which record that the rubric appears only in `OM_SSLG_2026_002.docx` and is omitted from copies (1) and (2) — i.e. the vault itself located the rubric in the No. 002 issuance alone. Half of a compound claim is unsupported, and it is the half that matters for a reader deciding which issuance is operative.

**I2 — 25 May memorandum note asserts execution ("Signed by …") that the file does not evidence.**
Note line `03 Governance/Memorandum No. 001, s. 2026 - Establishment of LGCC Standing Committees (25 May 2026).md:52` — "Signed by Hon. Rhian Jade L. Castro as LGCC Chairperson and SSLG President."
Measurement: the DOCX text carries the issuer only as a header field ("FROM: Hon. RHIAN JADE L. CASTRO" + the two title lines); there is no "/s/", no "signed"/"affix" marker and no signature block name paragraph — the file's last paragraphs are the EFFECTIVITY clause. The alternative-format PDF `03 Governance/Sources/OM_SSLG_2026_001.pdf` (8 pages, 14,235 extractable chars, "CASTRO" present) also has no "/s/" marker. Manifest `status` for this record is `final`, not `signed`.
The vault reserves `status: signed` (3 records) and "signed" wording for cases where a signed PDF exists (e.g. `nom-sslg-2026-004`, `om-sslg-2026-002-survey-aug28`, `om-nfsslg-2026-001`), so this key detail asserts certainty the source does not give. Titles themselves ("LGCC Chairperson", "SSLG President") are supported verbatim; only the act of signing is not.

**I3 — `om-nfsslg-2026-001` states a record date and a "signed" status taken from citations and the filename, not from the document it embeds.**
Note/manifest: `Record date: 2026-08-25` (`…NFSSLG….md:36`), `Document ID: OM-NFSSLG-2026-001`, `status: signed`, and the key details at `:47-48` ("image-only PDFs with no extractable text layer"; "The 16-page signed export … 17-page merged bundle … 2-page compressed extract").
Measurement: the embedded primary `04 External Memoranda/Sources/Memorandum-OM-NFSSLG-2026-001_signed-2.pdf` is 16 pages (`/Type /Page` = 16, `pdftotext` form feeds = 16) and `pdftotext … -` returns **16 characters total** (one per page) — no text layer, so the date, the number and the signatures are not machine-readable from the authority itself. The identifier plus the date "25 August 2026" is recoverable only from *other* documents in the collection that cite it: `04 External Memoranda/Sources/OM-DFSSLG-2026-010.pdf` (cites OM-NFSSLG-2026-001 as dated 25 August 2026), `04 External Memoranda/Sources/OM-CFSSLG-2026-003.docx` (cites it; that file's own DATE is September 1, 2026), and the school survey copies `(3)`/`(6).docx`. The note does disclose the image-only condition (good), but the At-a-glance block still presents the date and signed status as the document's own; per the spec these should be marked as attributed/cited. This is 1 of only 2 records of 36 whose `record_id` string does not appear in its primary text (the sweep: `ap-sslg-2026-003` is the other).

**I4 — Requiring mutual `related` links is the wrong rule for this vault.**
Measurement: `tools\tests\test_sslg_content.py::test_related_links_are_mutual` currently passes because 70 of 70 edges are mutual (my independent edge walk over `records[].related`: `related edges: 70 one-way edges: 0`; 7 records have no `related`). The rule is satisfiable today but it encodes a false claim about the collection: half of the edges are *disambiguation* edges created by the numbering collision, and a memorandum that never mentions another cannot cite it back. Forcing reciprocity makes the Obsidian graph render an apparent substantive relationship between, e.g., the ECOLEAD memoranda and the survey memoranda with no textual basis in either direction (measured: ECOLEAD copies → `survey=False digital=False`; survey copies → `ECOLEAD=False`). Recommended rule: keep bidirectional *navigation* (both notes can list each other) but store and render an edge type and direction (`cites` / `revises` / `same-number-conflict` / `shared-event`), and assert only that every edge's *stated basis* exists — not that the reverse edge exists. Under the present rule, a genuinely one-way citation (the ACR of Brigada Eskwela relies on the Division memorandum, which cannot know it) is indistinguishable from a mutual exchange.
