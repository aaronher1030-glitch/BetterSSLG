About

What this is

A prototype of a national registry and publishing standard for Supreme Secondary Learner Governments. One record per school produces one public site; the same records produce the directory, the activity feed, the issuance index and the transparency table.

It is modelled on BetterGov.ph: a volunteer-run, open-source portal built on a shared schema and a public repository, where the value is the consistency of the data rather than the design of any one page.

Records arrive through a form that runs in the browser (Submit). It validates the fields, shows the JSON as it is built, and downloads one file. Nothing is uploaded and no account is required; the school sends the file to its division.

Privacy rules

The officers of an SSLG are learners, which puts this platform under the Data Privacy Act of 2012 and the DepEd data privacy manual in a way that a government directory of adult officials is not.

  • Roles are public. Names are not.
  • A name renders only when a signed consent form is recorded against it.
  • No section names, no learner addresses, no personal mobile numbers.
  • No photograph of an identifiable learner without consent; event and document images are preferred.
  • A school can withdraw its record at any time, and the page goes down with it.

Verification

A record is published as Verified only after the adviser attests to it and the division confirms the school exists. Until then it carries a Demo record badge, which is visible on every page it touches. Without this step the platform would be a spoofing tool with a DepEd-shaped logo on it.

The record

One JSON file per school, six blocks:

BlockHolds
schoolName, school ID, division, region, cluster, address
sslgTerm, adviser, officers, contact
activitiesID, title, date, status, kind, summary, photo
documentsNumber, series, type, direction, title, date
financeFrequency, published reports
clubsName and cluster
